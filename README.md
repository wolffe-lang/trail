# trail

One wolf on a forest floor, and meat in the open. Eat, and another wolf runs in from beyond the edge to fall
in behind you; the pack is a single trail that grows with every meal. Eagles come for the last wolf in the
line, and whether they take it is a fair roll you can see. Leave by one edge of the screen and you arrive
from the other, still running. The third game of the lair, the wolf games collection, on the collection's
shared engine (`wolffe-lang/lair`); the others are `pack` and `cub`.
